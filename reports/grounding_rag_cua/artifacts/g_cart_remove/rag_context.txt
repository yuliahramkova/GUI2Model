5. **Confirm Deletion**: After clicking the delete option, confirm the deletion if prompted. This ensures that the item is removed from the cart.

6. **Verify Deletion**: Check the cart to ensure the item has been successfully removed. You can do this by clicking on the "My Cart" link again to refresh the cart view.
