To add a product to the comparison list on this storefront, several GUI elements, navigation paths, labels, roles, and actions will be relevant:

1. **GUI Elements**: 
   - **Product Image**: The product image is likely the first element users see, which helps them identify the product they're interested in.
   - **Product Title**: The title of the product, which is displayed prominently, provides the name and description of the product.
   - **Add to Comparison Button/Link**: This is the key element that allows users to add the product to their comparison list. It may be labeled as "Add to Compare," "Compare Now," or similar.
   - **Comparison List Link/Button**: This navigates to the comparison list where users can view the products they've added for comparison.

2. **Navigation Paths**:
   - **Product Detail Page**: Users typically arrive at the product detail page by clicking on a product title or a product link. This page contains all the necessary information about the product.
   - **Comparison List**: After adding the product to the comparison list, users can navigate back to the comparison list to review the products they've selected.

3. **Labels**:
   - **Product Title**: The title of the product, which is usually displayed at the top of the product detail page.
   - **Add to Comparison Label**: The label on the button or link that adds the product to the comparison list.
   - **Comparison List Label**: The label on the link or button that navigates to the comparison list.

4. **Roles**:
   - **User Role**: The user's role determines what actions they can perform. For example, a user might have the ability to add products to their comparison list but not edit or delete them.
   - **Product Role**: The role of the product itself, which might indicate its nature (e.g., a product that can be added to a comparison list).

5. **Actions**:
   - **Clicking the Product Title or Product Link**: This action takes the user to the product detail page.
   - **Clicking the Add to Comparison Button/Link**: This action adds the product to the comparison list.
   - **Navigating to the Comparison List**: This action allows the user to view the products they've added for comparison.

By focusing on these elements, labels, roles, and actions, users can effectively add a product to the comparison list on this storefront.
