To open your past orders while logged in, follow these steps:

1. **Navigate to the Customer Account Area**: Click on the "My Account" link typically located in the top right corner of the website. This link usually leads to a page where you can manage your account details, including viewing your order history.

2. **Access the Order History Page**: Once you are logged in, locate the "Order History" or "My Orders" link on the account management page. This link will take you to a page where you can view your past orders, including details like order numbers, dates, totals, and shipping information.

3. **Review Your Orders**: On the order history page, you can review the details of your past orders. This includes the order number, the date of the order, the total amount, and any shipping details.

By following these steps, you should be able to successfully open and view your past orders while logged into your account. The relevant navigation paths and labels may vary depending on the specific design of the website, but the general approach outlined above should help you find your order history efficiently.
