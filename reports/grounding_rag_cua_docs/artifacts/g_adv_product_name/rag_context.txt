To perform an Advanced Search and filter by product title, the following elements and actions are most relevant:

1. **GUI Elements**: 
   - **Search Field**: Click on the search field to begin typing your query.
   - **Advanced Search Button**: Locate the button labeled "Advanced Search" to access more filtering options.

2. **Navigation Paths**:
   - Start by navigating to the homepage.
   - From the homepage, locate the "Advanced Search" button in the header or the "Advanced Search" link on the homepage.

3. **Labels**:
   - **Advanced Search Label**: This label will guide you to the advanced search functionality.
   - **Search Field Label**: Typically labeled as "Search" or "Find Products."

4. **Roles**:
   - **User Role**: As a user, you need to interact with the search field and the advanced search button to perform the search.

5. **Actions**:
   - **Click on the Search Field**: This action initiates the search process.
   - **Type Query**: Enter the product title or keyword into the search field.
   - **Submit Search**: Click the "Search" button to execute the search.
   - **Select Advanced Search**: Click on the "Advanced Search" button to access additional filtering options.

By following these steps, you can effectively use the Advanced Search feature to filter products by title. The relevant sections and tasks mentioned in the context provide a structured approach to navigating the Advanced Search interface and executing the search.
