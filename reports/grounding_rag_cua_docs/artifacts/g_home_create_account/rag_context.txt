To register a new customer account from the header of the storefront, the following GUI elements, navigation paths, labels, roles, and actions are most relevant:

1. **GUI Elements**: 
   - The "Header" section, which typically contains navigation links and possibly a login or register link.
   - The "Customer account" link, which usually leads to the customer account management page.

2. **Navigation Paths**:
   - From the "Header," navigate to the "Customer account" section.
   - Follow the link labeled "Register" or "Create an Account."

3. **Labels**:
   - The label "Customer account" is crucial as it directs users to the account management section.
   - The label "Register" or "Create an Account" is the call-to-action for new users to create a new account.

4. **Roles**:
   - The role of a "customer" is relevant here, as they are the ones who would initiate the registration process.

5. **Actions**:
   - Click on the "Customer account" link.
   - Click on the "Register" or "Create an Account" link to proceed with the registration process.

By following these steps, a user can navigate to the registration page and complete the necessary actions to create a new customer account.
