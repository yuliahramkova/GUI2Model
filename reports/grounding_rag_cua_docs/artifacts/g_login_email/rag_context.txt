To sign in to your account on the storefront, you'll need to follow these steps:

1. **Navigation Path**: Start by navigating to the login page. The URL for the login page is typically `/customer/account/login/`, which is a common endpoint for customer accounts on many e-commerce platforms like Magento.

2. **GUI Elements**: On the login page, look for the following elements:
   - **Email Input Field**: This is where you would enter your email address. It is usually labeled as "Email" or "Username".
   - **Password Input Field**: After entering your email, you'll need to provide your password. This field is usually labeled as "Password".

3. **Labels**: The labels for the fields are crucial for understanding what needs to be entered. Common labels include:
   - "Email" or "Username"
   - "Password"

4. **Roles**: As a customer, you are the user role interacting with the storefront. You need to log in to manage your account details, view orders, and access other account-related features.

5. **Actions**: The primary action required is to click the "Login" button after entering your email and password. This button is usually labeled as "Login", "Sign In", or something similar.

By following these steps and interacting with the appropriate GUI elements, you should be able to successfully sign in to your account on the storefront.
