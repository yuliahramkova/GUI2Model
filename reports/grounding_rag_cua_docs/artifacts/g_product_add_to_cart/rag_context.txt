To put a product into the cart on the storefront, several key elements and actions are relevant:

1. **GUI Elements**: 
   - **Add to Cart Button**: This button is typically labeled as "Add to Cart" or something similar. It is the primary action required to add a product to the shopping cart.
   - **Minicart (My Cart)**: This is a small cart icon usually located in the header of the website. Clicking this opens the minicart dropdown, which allows you to view and manage items in your cart.
   - **Product Page**: This is where the product details, including the "Add to Cart" button, are displayed. It is the starting point for adding a product to the cart.

2. **Navigation Paths**:
   - **From Product Page to Cart**: After clicking the "Add to Cart" button on the product page, you should navigate to the shopping cart page. The URL typically ends with `/checkout/cart/` and the title should contain "Shopping Cart".

3. **Labels**:
   - **Add to Cart Label**: The label on the "Add to Cart" button should clearly indicate what it does, such as "Add to Cart".
   - **Minicart Label**: The label for the minicart icon in the header should be something like "My Cart" or "Cart".

4. **Roles**:
   - **Customer Role**: As a customer, you would interact with these elements to add a product to your cart.

5. **Actions**:
   - **Click the "Add to Cart" Button**: This is the primary action needed to add a product to the cart.
   - **Open Minicart Dropdown**: If a spinner appears while adding the product, you should wait for 2000 milliseconds. Then, click the "View and Edit Cart" mark in the minicart dropdown to ensure you're navigating to the correct cart page.
   - **Navigate to Shopping Cart**: Ensure the URL contains `/checkout/cart/` and the title contains "Shopping Cart" to confirm you are on the correct page.

By following these steps, you can successfully add a product to the cart on the storefront.
