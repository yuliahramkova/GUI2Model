To add a product to the comparison list on a storefront, several GUI elements, navigation paths, labels, roles, and actions are relevant. Here’s a breakdown of what you would typically encounter:

### GUI Elements and Navigation Paths:
1. **Product Page**: This is where the product details are displayed, including the product title, price, description, images, and options.
2. **Comparison List**: This is usually a section where multiple products can be compared side by side. It might be labeled as "Compare," "Compare Products," or "Compare List."
3. **Add to Comparison Button**: This button allows you to add the selected product to the comparison list. It could be labeled as "Add to Compare," "Compare Now," or simply "Compare."

### Labels and Roles:
1. **Product Title**: The name of the product, which helps identify the item you're interested in comparing.
2. **Price**: The cost of the product, which is crucial when comparing different options.
3. **Description**: A brief overview of the product features and specifications.
4. **Images**: Visual representations of the product, which can provide a better understanding of the item.
5. **Options**: Any additional configurations or variations of the product, like color, size, or quantity.
6. **Comparison List Label**: The label for the section where you can compare products, such as "Compare List" or "Compare Products."

### Actions:
1. **Click on Product**: To view the product details.
2. **Click on "Add to Comparison" Button**: To add the product to the comparison list.
3. **Select Options**: If the product has configurable options, choose the desired ones before adding it to the comparison list.
4. **Review and Adjust**: After adding the product, review the comparison list to ensure the correct product is included and adjust as necessary.

### Typical Actions Associated with Magento:
- **Navigate**: Use the navigation menu to find the product category.
- **Browse Category**: Browse through the categories to locate the product.
- **Open Product Detail Page**: Click on the product title or image to view the product details.
- **Add to Comparison**: Click the "Add to Compare" button to add the product to the comparison list.

By following these steps, you can effectively add a product to the comparison list on a storefront. Ensure that you are logged in if the site requires authentication to access the comparison list.
