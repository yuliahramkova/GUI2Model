2. **GUI Elements**:

   - The label for the "Advanced Search" button or link, which might say "Advanced Search".

4. **Roles**:
   - The role of the user performing the search, which is likely a customer or a visitor looking to find a specific product by its SKU.

5. **Actions**:
   - Clicking the "Advanced Search" button or link to open the advanced search form.
   - Typing the SKU into the search field.
