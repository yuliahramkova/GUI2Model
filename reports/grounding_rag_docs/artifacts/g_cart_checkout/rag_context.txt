To continue from the cart page to checkout, the following GUI elements, navigation paths, labels, roles, and actions are most relevant:

1. **GUI Elements**:

   - **"Checkout" Button**: This button is usually present on the cart page and directs you to the checkout process.

2. **Navigation Paths**:
   - Click on the "View and Edit Cart" button to navigate to the full cart page.
   - From the cart page, click on the "Checkout" button to proceed to the checkout process.

3. **Labels**:
   - The "View and Edit Cart" label indicates the button that leads to the detailed cart page.
   - The "Checkout" label is the button that initiates the checkout process.

4. **Roles**:
   - As a user, you would interact with these elements to manage your cart and proceed to checkout.

5. **Actions**:
   - Click on the "View and Edit Cart" button to view the cart details.
   - Click on the "Checkout" button to start the checkout process.
