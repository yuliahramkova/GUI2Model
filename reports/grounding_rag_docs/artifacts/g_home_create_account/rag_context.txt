To register a new customer account on the storefront, the following GUI elements, navigation paths, labels, roles, and actions are most relevant:

1. **GUI Elements**: 
   - The "Sign In" button or link, typically located in the header or top right corner of the page.
   - The "Register" or "Create an Account" link, usually found near the "Sign In" button or in a separate registration section.

2. **Navigation Paths**:
   - The path to reach the registration page might involve clicking on the "Register" or "Create an Account" link, which could lead to a dedicated registration form.

3. **Labels**:
   - Labels on the registration form will guide the user through the process, such as "First Name," "Last Name," "Email," "Password," etc.

4. **Roles**:
   - The role of the user is a "customer" who is looking to create a new account.

5. **Actions**:
   - The user needs to perform actions like clicking the "Register" or "Create an Account" button, filling out the registration form with personal information, and confirming the registration by possibly clicking a confirmation link sent to the email address provided.

By following these steps, the user can successfully register a new account on the storefront.
