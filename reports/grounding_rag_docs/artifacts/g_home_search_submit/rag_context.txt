To submit the header search after entering keywords on the storefront, the following elements and actions are most relevant:

1. **GUI Elements**: 
   - **Search Field**: This is the input field where keywords are entered.
   - **Submit Button**: This button is typically labeled as "Search" or "Submit" and is used to initiate the search after entering keywords.

2. **Navigation Paths**:
   - **Home Page**: The starting point where the user navigates to the storefront.
   - **Search Path**: The sequence of actions leading to the search submission, which includes entering keywords in the search field and clicking the submit button.

3. **Labels**:
   - **Search Field Label**: Usually labeled as "Search" or "Find Products".
   - **Submit Button Label**: Typically labeled as "Search" or "Submit".

4. **Roles**:
   - **User**: The role of the person performing the search action.

5. **Actions**:
   - **Clicking the Search Field**: Entering keywords into the search field.
   - **Clicking the Submit Button**: Initiating the search process.

These elements and actions are crucial for navigating through the storefront and performing a search effectively. The user would first click on the search field, enter the desired keywords, and then click the submit button to execute the search.
