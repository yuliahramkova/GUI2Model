To navigate from the open mini-cart to the checkout process, the following GUI elements, navigation paths, labels, roles, and actions are most relevant:

1. **GUI Elements**:
   - **Mini-Cart Icon**: This is typically a small icon representing the shopping cart, usually located in the header of the website.
   - **Mini-Cart Label**: The label next to the mini-cart icon, such as "My Cart" or "View and Edit Cart," indicates the name of the cart.
   - **Checkout Button**: This button is usually labeled "Proceed to Checkout," "Check Out," or something similar, and it is prominently displayed on the mini-cart page.

2. **Navigation Paths**:
   - **Click on Mini-Cart Icon**: This action opens the mini-cart.
   - **Navigate to Checkout Page**: After opening the mini-cart, click on the "Proceed to Checkout" button to proceed to the checkout page.

3. **Labels**:
   - **Mini-Cart Label**: Labels like "My Cart" or "View and Edit Cart" help identify the mini-cart.
   - **Checkout Button Label**: The label "Proceed to Checkout" or similar indicates the button to click to move to the checkout process.

4. **Roles**:
   - **Customer**: The role of the user is to manage their shopping cart and proceed to checkout.

5. **Actions**:
   - **Open Mini-Cart**: Click on the mini-cart icon to view the items currently in the cart.
   - **Click on Checkout Button**: Once the mini-cart is open, click on the "Proceed to Checkout" button to start the checkout process.

By following these steps, the user can efficiently transition from viewing their mini-cart to completing the checkout process.
