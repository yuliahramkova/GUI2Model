1. **Navigate to the Guest Order Lookup Form**: You can find the guest order lookup form by clicking on the link provided in the Knowledge Graph Data, which is `/sales/guest/form/`.

2. **Identify the Navigation Path**: The navigation path to reach the guest order lookup form might involve:
   - Clicking on the "Orders and Returns" section in the footer of the website.
   - Alternatively, you may find a link labeled "Look Up Order" or something similar in the footer or main navigation menu.

3. **Locate the Form Elements**: Once you navigate to the guest order lookup form, you'll likely encounter fields such as:
   - An input field where you can enter your order number.
   - A button labeled "Submit" or "Lookup".

4. **Input the Order Number**: Enter the order number you want to look up in the designated input field.

5. **Submit the Form**: After entering the order number, click the "Submit" or "Lookup" button to proceed.

6. **Review the Results**: The form will display the order details, including the order number, total cost, and other relevant information.

By following these steps, you can effectively use the guest order lookup form to retrieve information about your order without needing to sign in.
